# N5: proposed full algorithm, 28 September 2026, approximately 12:18–12:20 UTC

Unverified lead, not yet a claim. The previous turn was concrete progress on N8. This new route aims at the full torsion case of N5, rather than only another torsion-free special case. No Kourovka mathematics or code is used.

## 1. Finite reduction modulo the actual centre

Let G be finitely generated nilpotent, Z=Z(G), T=T(G), Q=G/T. Compute these groups and a rational Malcev completion R of Q. Use the established algorithm for a decomposition of R into rationally indecomposable factors R_1,...,R_m.

The ingredient from Baumslag–Miller–Ostheimer, arXiv:1510.05632, Proposition 18 and Corollary 19, is that every rational direct decomposition matches a partition of these factors modulo Z(R). Thus any G=A x B determines a partition whose two supports modulo Z(R) agree with those of A and B.

Put K=G/Z and S=T(K). The kernel of G/Z -> R/Z(R) is S: an element whose rational image is central has a power in Z(G), and conversely. A direct proof/algorithm for this equality must be written. In particular S is finite.

For every partition, form K_i as the preimage in K of the corresponding rational support R_I Z(R)/Z(R). If an actual decomposition G=A x B matches it, then Y_1=AZ/Z and Y_2=BZ/Z satisfy K=Y_1 x Y_2 and K_i=Y_i S. Consequently [K_i:Y_i]<=|S|. There are finitely many subgroups of these bounded indices in each finitely generated K_i, and they can be effectively enumerated by permutation representations. Retain only pairs actually giving K=Y_1 x Y_2. Include empty partitions and trivial factors: a central direct factor has trivial image in K.

This supplies a finite complete list of decompositions modulo Z. It avoids any unproved finiteness assertion about all decompositions of Q up to automorphism.

## 2. Central lifting as abelian constraints

For a retained pair let X_i be its full preimage in G. Test [X_1,X_2]=1 on finite generators; this is necessary and unaffected by central choices. Also X_1 X_2=G and X_1 intersect X_2=Z.

Choose a finite presentation of Y_i and generator lifts x_ij in X_i. Every relator evaluates to a central defect v_il in Z. Let E_i be the matrix of exponent sums of the relators. Changing lifts by z_j in Z changes the defect vector by E_i z.

A central splitting Z=Z_1 direct-sum Z_2 yields the required group splitting iff the central extension X_i/Z_i -> Y_i splits. This means the defects can be killed modulo Z_i by such changes of lifts. Smith reduction of E_i turns this into:

- finitely many exact containments v in Z_i (zero Smith rows);
- finitely many congruence containments v in Z_i+dZ (positive Smith diagonal d).

If these conditions hold, solve the finite abelian linear systems to construct sections and take their preimages in X_i. The factors commute, intersect trivially and generate G. Require each resulting factor to be nontrivial (Y_i !=1 or Z_i !=0).

This step retains all relator defects. Arbitrary fixed lifts of a polycyclic quotient sequence need not give a subgroup whose central intersection fits the desired central factor; that shortcut must not be used.

## 3. Deciding the central constraints in a finitely generated abelian group

Write Z=T_0 direct-sum F, where T_0 is finite and F=Z^n. Enumerate all direct splittings T_0=T_1 direct-sum T_2 and all shears beta in Hom(F,T_0), a finite set. Every direct splitting of Z is obtained from one of the product splittings (T_1 direct-sum A),(T_2 direct-sum B), F=A direct-sum B, by such a shear. Apply the inverse shear to the constraint vectors. Torsion parts can then be tested finitely and separately.

The exact free-part containments require A>=W_1, B>=W_2, where W_i is the saturation of the lattice generated by its exact-constraint vectors. Necessarily W_1 intersect W_2=0 and W_1+W_2 is primitive in F. If either condition fails, reject this shear/torsion split. Otherwise choose F=W_1 direct-sum W_2 direct-sum C.

Every free splitting containing W_i is the image of

    A_k=W_1 direct-sum <c_1,...,c_k>,
    B_k=W_2 direct-sum <c_(k+1),...,c_d>,  0<=k<=d,

under the explicitly finitely generated group Gamma of automorphisms fixing W_1+W_2 pointwise. In this basis its matrices are [[I,0,U],[0,I,V],[0,0,W]], W in GL_d(Z). Elementary shears, signed permutations and elementary matrices give generators.

Let D be the lcm of the positive Smith diagonals (D=1 if no nontrivial congruence). The remaining conditions depend only on Gamma modulo D. Enumerate its finite image, retaining words and hence integral lifts. For each image and each k, test the congruence containments. Acceptance constructs the central splitting, failure of the finite enumeration proves nonexistence. Filter trivial factors using ranks and torsion sizes.
