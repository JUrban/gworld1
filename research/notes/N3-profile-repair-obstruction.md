# N3: an exact cover criterion and an obstruction to enlarging local bounds

29 September 2026. Supplementary progress on the failed published
construction, **not a solution to N3** and not an additional candidate.
The full original `sources/raw/probnil.html` and the actual N3 rendering
were inspected again. The question has no cardinality restriction.

The source and earlier counterexample are documented in
[the published-cover audit](N3-published-cover-audit.md). The relevant
construction is Definition 3.1 of Göbel–Shelah–Wallutis (2003),
[published author copy](https://shelah.logic.at/files/203632/Sh-742.pdf).
Their unrestricted torsion-freeness proposition is false. Simply
factoring out the resulting torsion need not preserve the map to the
intended target. This note makes that obstruction precise.

## 1. What a repair actually needs

Let M index a generating family of a locally nilpotent group G, let
F=F(M), and let pi:F -> G be the specified epimorphism. For an increasing
positive integer profile c on nonempty finite subsets of M, write

    K(c) = normal_closure_F { gamma_(c(U)+1)(F(U)) : U finite },
    P(c) = F/K(c).

Choose c so that each subgroup generated by pi(U) has class at most c(U).
Then K(c) is contained in ker(pi). For any increasing d>=c there is a
canonical epimorphism P(d) -> G. The following are equivalent:

1. G is an epimorphic image of a torsion-free locally nilpotent group.
2. For some increasing d>=c, the canonical map P(d) -> G kills every
   torsion element of P(d).
3. For some such d, the isolator

       sqrt(K(d)) = { w in F : w^n in K(d) for some n>=1 }

   is contained in ker(pi).

Here the isolator is a subgroup because P(d) is locally nilpotent.
Indeed, torsion elements of a locally nilpotent group form a normal
subgroup T: any finitely many of them generate a nilpotent group
generated by torsion elements, hence a finite group. The quotient by T
is torsion-free, since x^n in T implies x^(nk)=1 for some k>=1.

For (1) -> (2), lift each specified generator of G to a torsion-free
locally nilpotent cover H, and replace H by the subgroup generated by
these lifts. Let d(U) be the maximum of c(U) and the nilpotency class
of the corresponding subgroup of lifts. This is an increasing finite
profile. The free-generator map factors through P(d) -> H -> G;
torsion maps trivially to H. Conversely, (2) gives an epimorphism
P(d)/Tor(P(d)) -> G from a torsion-free locally nilpotent group.
The equivalence with (3) is immediate from the definition of torsion.

In particular, N3 is equivalent to the following profile statement:

> For every M and every increasing c, there exists increasing d>=c
> such that sqrt(K(d)) is contained in K(c).

Necessity applies the preceding argument to G=P(c); sufficiency first
maps P(c) onto the desired G. This does not assert P(d) itself is
torsion-free, a stronger and unnecessary requirement.

The condition is local **for a fixed globally compatible d**. For every
finite U, P(U,d|U) embeds in P(M,d): sending generators outside U to
the identity induces a retraction. To check this, the image of a relator
from V lies in gamma_(d(V)+1)(F(U intersect V)), which is killed since
d(U intersect V)<=d(V). Thus a torsion element is already a torsion
element of some finite-coordinate group, and condition (2) can be
checked on these groups. Selecting unrelated profiles separately for
each finite U does not supply a single such d.

For finite M the criterion is easy: set d(U)=c(M) for every nonempty U.
Then P(d) is the free nilpotent group of class c(M), so it is torsion-free.
The difficult part of N3 remains the coherent choice for arbitrary M.
This reformulation is elementary; no novelty is asserted for it.

## 2. Pair bounds 2 -> 3 do not suffice with full bound six

Use a,b,c as free generators and the convention [x,y]=x^-1 y^-1 x y;
multiple brackets are left-associated. Let

    H(b,C) = F(a,b,c) / normal_closure(
                 gamma_(C+1)(F(a,b,c)),
                 gamma_(b+1)(F(a,b)),
                 gamma_(b+1)(F(a,c)),
                 gamma_(b+1)(F(b,c))).

There is a natural map H(3,6) -> H(2,6). We exhibit an element z of
order two in H(3,6) whose image also has order two in H(2,6).
Consequently this map does not factor through H(3,6)/Tor(H(3,6)).

Put s=[b,a], t=[c,a], u=[c,b]. In the free class-six group define z
as the product of the following central weight-six factors:

    [[t,b],[s,c]]^-2  [[t,c],[s,b]]^-1  [[u,c],[s,a]]^-1
    [[u,s],t]^-2  [s,a,c,u]  [s,b,c,t]^2  [s,c,c,s]
    [t,a,b,u]^-1  [t,b,b,t]^-1  [t,b,c,s]^-2
    [t,a,b,b,c]^-1.

The integral identity

    z^2 = [b,a,b,a,c,c]^-1
          [c,a,c,a,b,b]^-1
          [c,b,c,b,a,a]^-1                             (1)

holds in the free class-six group. Each factor on the right is a
conjugation difference of a defining pair-class-three relator. Thus
z^2=1 in H(3,6), and also in H(2,6).

The nontriviality proof in H(2,6) uses an explicit integer certificate,
described next. This also proves nontriviality in H(3,6). The certificate
stores a 538-letter free word with precisely this Magnus expansion;
its eleven-factor expression is independently parsed and checked.

## 3. Independent certificate for nontriviality

Work in the integral noncommutative polynomial ring on A,B,C truncated
above degree six, with a -> 1+A and similarly for b,c. The standard
free-group Magnus embedding identifies the free class-six group with
its image in these units. In particular equality of these expansions
certifies (1); no quotient word-problem oracle is used for that identity.

Let J be the polynomials supported in degrees at least three. The group
1+J has class at most two: J^3=0 and all commutators lie in degree six,
which is central. The six pair-class-two relators have their images in
1+J. Their normal closure in the Magnus image will be denoted K.

The certificate supplies 180 elements B_i in 1+J, ordered by their
first nonzero nonconstant monomial in degree-then-lexicographic order.
These pivots are distinct and their integer coefficients are positive.
For each pair of rows the commutator either vanishes by degree or
collects as an integral product of the rows with degree-six pivots.
Therefore all ordered products of integral powers of the B_i form a
subgroup B. Reordering causes only the verified central corrections.

An element belongs to B exactly when sequential collection through
these pivots reaches the identity: at pivot p_i its coefficient must
be divisible by that of B_i, determining the exponent uniquely. Later
rows and multiplication corrections cannot affect this pivot. This
gives both sound membership and sound nonmembership tests.

The standalone verifier checks:

- all 15 potentially nontrivial commutators of the degree-three rows;
- all 1080 conjugates B_i^(a^eps), B_i^(b^eps), B_i^(c^eps), eps=+/-1,
  belong to B;
- all 240 pair-relator conjugation differences through depth three
  belong to B, including the six defining relators;
- the expansion of z does not belong to B. Collection reaches monomial
  **ABCACB** with coefficient **-2**, whereas the pivot divisor is **4**.

The signed conjugation checks make B invariant under the free generators
and their inverses. Since B contains the six relators, K is contained
in B. Hence z is not in K. We do not need, or assume, that B equals K.
Together with (1) this proves both asserted orders are exactly two.

The first attempted certificate used an additive mod-two envelope and
failed. That failure was retained. The successful certificate preserves
the multiplicative degree-six corrections and integer divisibility;
it does not mistake failure of a mod-two separator for triviality.

## 4. Even increasing every bound by one does not suffice

Take the profile c with singleton bounds one, pair bounds two and full
bound six. The profile d(U)=c(U)+1 gives singleton bounds two, pair bounds
three and full bound seven. The singleton conditions are automatic for
cyclic coordinate groups. Thus the canonical map is H(3,7) -> H(2,6).
It too has a torsion element with a nontrivial image.

Write N=F(a,b,c)/gamma_8, and let z0 be the specific 538-letter lift of
the class-six element z stored in the certificate. The eleven-factor
expression above is asserted equal to it modulo gamma_7; we do not
silently identify their possible degree-seven corrections. There is
an explicit central word q in gamma_7(N) such that

    Z = z0^3 q

has order two in H(3,7). Its image in H(2,6) is z^3=z, which is nontrivial
by the independent certificate above. This proves failure of the proposed
uniform increment and subsequent torsion quotient.

The class-seven certificate gives q as a product of 56 degree-seven Hall
words with integer exponents. It gives Z^2 as a product of 24 nonzero
powers of specified defining relators and their conjugation differences.
The standalone verifier expands both sides over the integers, verifies
that every correction term has pure degree seven, and reruns the entire
class-six nonmembership certificate. GAP independently checks the fixed
word in both presented quotients and the positive identity in the free
class-seven group. No centrality of Z in H(3,7) is asserted.

Here is how q was found. The twelve pair-class-three relators lie in
gamma_4(N), an abelian subgroup because gamma_8(N)=1. Their normal closure
therefore has an exact additive Magnus lattice generated by the 480
relators and iterated conjugation differences through depth three.
Conjugation minus identity raises degree, and its fourth power vanishes;
the finite geometric series also accounts for inverse conjugations.
An integral Hermite transform, checked together with its unimodular
change-of-basis matrix, supplies the lattice's central degree-seven rows.

Let R denote the positive expansion of the right side of (1), minus one,
and let v be the degree-seven-truncated expansion of z0, minus one.
Then e=6v-3R is homogeneous of degree seven. A binary solve in the central
rows finds h in the normal-relator lattice with h congruent to e modulo
two. The tensor (h-e)/2 is then explicitly converted to an **integral**
degree-seven Hall vector; this conversion is checked, not assumed from
parity alone. Take q with that vector. Since all relevant products start
in degrees at least six, the equality

    2(3v + (h-e)/2) = 3R + h

is exactly the recorded positive group identity for Z^2. The final
verifiers do not trust the Hermite solver, its parity solve or the Hall
conversion: they check the resulting words and integer coefficients.

bounds, not on unrestricted torsion-free covers.

## 5. Limits and retained failures

Neither example is a counterexample to N3: the target H(2,6) has the
obvious torsion-free cover F(a,b,c)/gamma_7. The remaining problem is
to find a generally valid coherent profile enlargement or an entirely
different cover construction. No arbitrary enlargement rule is excluded
by the present examples. There is no additional solution or novelty count.

The broader first probe completed the (2,5), (3,5), (3,6), and (3,7)
source cases mapping to pair-bound-two targets with the same full bound.
It timed out after 180 seconds during (3,8); the planned pair-bound-four
cases were never reached. Its partial output is not a completed sweep.
The probe also emitted a forward-global GAP syntax warning.

The first class-six integer constructor failed to find an additive
mod-two separator. The replacement multiplicative certificate passed.
The first final GAP check passed but emitted a forward-global warning;
its source is preserved and the corrected check passes with empty stderr.

The uniform-increment GAP probe found a torsion element of order six
with an order-two image, but its expanded preimage has 277662 letters.
An attempted Hall-coordinate shortening reached GAP's preset memory
limit. GAP exited zero, but the expected completion marker was absent,
so the recorded runner correctly classified the run as failed. Increasing
the memory limit was unnecessary: the independent lattice construction
above produced the compact order-two witness directly.

As an additional negative control, the uncorrected 538-letter lift z0 has
infinite order in H(3,7). It cannot be substituted for Z. The successful
uniform-increment certificate retains its central correction and the
cube of its prefix explicitly.

All mathematical commands used the recorded runner, at most two cores
and 18 GB combined requested memory. No random choices, subagents,
author contact, push, or Kourovka mathematical transfer were used.
The new searches for N3 cover constructions and corrections did not
locate a repair; this is a bounded literature check, not a novelty claim.
