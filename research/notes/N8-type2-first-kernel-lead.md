# Type-(2,q) first-correction lead

29 September 2026. Written follow-up to the type-one audit; not yet promoted
or computationally checked. This is a proposed extension of the same N8
partial candidate, not an answer to all targets in arbitrary class.

Let L be a free rational Lie algebra on degree-one generators. Fix nonzero
C in L2 and D in Lq, q>3, and put delta=ad_C. The proposed assertions are:

    ker( L3 + L_(q+1) -> L_(q+3),
         (U,V) |-> [U,D]+delta V ) has dimension at most one;

and for a nonzero kernel direction (U,-V),

    [U,V] not in [L4,D]+delta L_(q+2).                  (*)

These would give the same finite quadratic lifting algorithm for type(2,q)
in class c=q+4 as the type(1,q) procedure in type1-third-proof.md.

## Proposed free-chain reduction

Choose a homogeneous free generating set of L', with C as one of its
weight-two generators. This uses the homogeneous Shirshov facts already
credited in class9-proof.md. Let J be the ideal generated by every other
free generator. The usual elimination construction makes J free on

    delta^j s, j>=0, s any chosen generator other than C.

One may also prove this directly in the free associative algebra by leading
words s*C^j with a suitable order, together with the derivation rule for
spanning; this is the standard free-Lie elimination argument. Here delta
raises original weight by two. The quotient L'/J is the one-dimensional
Lie algebra on C, concentrated in weight two. Consequently D and both
correction spaces in the displayed map lie in J.

Every U in L3 is a linear combination of the weight-three free seeds of J:
no decomposable word or positive delta-shift has weight three. A nonzero
U can therefore be selected as a seed by a rational change of basis.
The endpoint-polynomial/Lyndon support lemma in N8-general-first-kernel.md
applies without any dependence on the original weights. It forces D,V
into the U-chain. Two independent U directions force D=0. Delta is
injective on J, so the first-kernel dimension is at most one.

## Proposed quadratic obstruction

Project J onto its U-chain, killing all other chains. This commutes with
delta. Its image on L4 is zero: single chain letters have weights3,5,7,...,
and words of length at least two have weight at least six. Thus any
membership contrary to (*) would give delta W=[U,V] inside this chain.

Embed the chain in Lie(z,t) by delta^j U -> ad_z^j(t), now giving z weight2
and t weight3. The cyclic-word double-primitive lemma is independent of
these weights. D has weight q>3, so has no ordinary word-length-one part.
The two equations [z,V]=[t,D] and [z,W]=[t,V] would force D=0, a
contradiction. This proves the proposed obstruction, subject to checking
the free-chain reduction carefully.

## Integral and boundary issues still to check

The q=3 case is excluded: D may be a scalar multiple of U and V=0.
Its kernel is the exact Nielsen direction x -> y*x, with a finite integer
period after leading scales are fixed; the earlier class-seven argument
already treats that case. Equal type(2,2) belongs to earlier low-class work.

For q>3, retain every signed integral leading scale. First corrections have
weights3,q+1 and final corrections4,q+2. Their only quadratic interaction
has weight q+4=c; interactions with two weight-three corrections have
weight at least q+6. The same integral affine-line and final Hermite
procedure should therefore terminate. Other leading types must remain
unsupported unless separately handled.

A decisive group control is C=[b,a], U=[a,C], D=ad_C^2(U), of weights2,3,7.
Then V=[U,[C,U]] and delta V=[U,D]. Its target has leading degree9 in
class11; this is an exceptional type(2,7) branch outside the type-one
solver. Verify its complete leading types, primitive affine line, nonzero
final cokernel and perturbed negative cases independently in GAP before
promoting scope. No computation for this lead has yet been run.
