# O6: the primitive-extension reduction is not a full resolution

29 September 2026, around 06:57--07:00 UTC. Literature scope check only.

The full frozen `sources/raw/probout.html` and the complete O6 background
paragraph in `sources/raw/Back.html` were read. The original question asks
whether every one-relator group without Baumslag--Solitar subgroups is
hyperbolic. Its archived background already gives the torsion case and
weaker CSA consequences. A fresh visual capture was attempted but Chromium
could not load libatk-1.0.so.0; no visual inspection is claimed here.

Marco Linton, *Hyperbolic one-relator groups*, Canadian Journal of
Mathematics 78(1) (2026), 35--61, published online in 2024, has an explicit
reduction rather than a full affirmative answer. The publisher's full
HTML was opened and archived; Theorem 5.6 and its short proof, together
with Corollary 5.7, were read. The theorem says that hyperbolicity of every
primitive extension subgroup suffices. The corollary reduces Gersten's
conjecture to those groups. The introduction explicitly leaves their
remaining hyperbolicity classification open. No theorem here removes that
remaining hypothesis. The implication cannot be applied merely from
absence of Baumslag--Solitar subgroups in the ambient group.
