# B9 fixed-first-color reduction

30 September 2026, within the original research window.

The previous permutation reductions do not cover all underlying strands.
Instead use the already proved rigidity of nontrivial special braids
modulo right B2 cosets. If a S(c) and a S(d) both belong to B3, then
S(c^-1 d) belongs to B3, hence c^-1 d belongs to B2. For c,d nontrivial
special, rigidity forces equality. If one is trivial, a belongs to B3
and the only possible second colors are 1 and sigma1.

This should exclude all first colors on the four known terminal-pair
rays, for arbitrary special second colors and arbitrary total parameter
exponent. It would strengthen the older check of one selected cancelling
partner in the Fibonacci-type recurrence, but would not exhaust B4.
