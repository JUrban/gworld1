# GA5(c): prior positive existential answer

29 September 2026. **Prior scope, not a new candidate.**

The frozen original asks whether a group with a simple index-two virtual
endomorphism can be free of rank k>1. Here simple means that no nontrivial
subgroup K of the domain, normal in the whole group G, satisfies f(K)<=K.
It does not mean that every invariant subgroup is trivial, as required
in the separate F28 question. The complete original group-actions HTML,
linked GA5 background and actual GA5 rendering have been inspected.

Nekrashevych, *Self-similar groups* (published 2005), Section 1.10.4,
printed pages 26--27 of the archived book manuscript, gives a faithful
self-similar action of F2 on the binary rooted tree. With x=ab and y=bc
in the preceding faithful C2*C2*C2 action, the recursions are

    x = sigma(x^-1, y),     y = (xy, y^-1 x^-1).

The subgroup generated by x,y has index two in C2*C2*C2 and is free of
rank two. This index is distinct from the first-level stabilizer index
used next. The preceding Theorem 1.10.2 credits E. Muntyan and D. Savchuk
for the free-product example.

For completeness, the precise bridge to GA5 is as follows. Let H be the
stabilizer of the vertex 0 in F2. Since x interchanges 0 and 1, H has
index two. Taking the section at 0 defines a homomorphism f:H->F2.
Suppose K<=H is normal in F2 and f(K)<=K. Every element of K fixes the
first level. Its section at 0 belongs to K by hypothesis; its section
at 1 belongs to K by conjugating with x and using normality (the section
of x which occurs in the formula also belongs to F2). Thus every section
of every K-element lies in K. Induction on levels shows that every such
element fixes every vertex. Faithfulness forces K=1. Hence f is simple.

This answers the existential wording of (c) positively with k=2. We
make no all-ranks statement from this source alone. It does not answer
the classification requested in (a), and it is not the stronger F28
non-normal-invariant-subgroup construction. Part (b)'s separate prior
Bartholdi--Sidki answer is unchanged. No result count increases.
