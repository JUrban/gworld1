29 September 2026. Neither question is solved here. The full original

## F23: the ambient rank is two

Kleiman, *Some questions in the theory of varieties of groups*,
Math. USSR-Izvestiya22(1984),33--65, gives a higher-rank construction.
Corollary8.11 on printedp64 states the strictly increasing chain for
rank89; the following sentence reports a reduction to73. The introduction
onp36 explicitly distinguishes the rank-two question. Those pages were
read and visually inspected, and the original PDF is archived. The full
33-page proof and the additional rank73 argument were not audited.
[Primary paper](https://www.mathnet.ru/php/getFT.phtml?jrnid=im&paperid=1381&what=fullteng).

This does not answer F23. An arbitrary embedding of a higher-rank free
group into F2 does not preserve its entire endomorphism action. Transporting
words through such an embedding therefore does not automatically transport
containments between their fully invariant closures, or their strictness.
No rank-reducing construction was established in this pass. The dated
statement of openness is not certification of current openness.

## S4: commutator width, for every derived length

Bardakov--Gongopadhyay, *Palindromic Width of Finitely Generated Solvable
Groups*, arXiv:1402.6115v1, introductionp2, explicitly credits Rhemtulla's
finite commutator-width theorem in derived length three and distinguishes
the higher-derived-length question. Its main theorems concern palindromic
width under the stated restrictions. The introduction, Theorems1.1--1.2,
Corollary1.3, Lemma2.1 and the relevant bibliography were read; p2 was
actually viewed. The complete proofs were not audited.
[Primary preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/1402.6115v1).

The original GroupWorld background already credits the metabelian and
derived-length-three cases. Neither the abelian-kernel commutator formula
nor a theorem about free solvable **Lie algebras** supplies the required
all-derived-length group theorem. No such extension was proved here.
