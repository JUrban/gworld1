# E6: strong conciseness does not settle equational Noetherianity

29 September 2026. **No solution or candidate from this literature lead.**

The frozen equations HTML was read in full and the actual E6 rendering
viewed. E6 asks whether a free pro-p group is equationally Noetherian:
every system of group equations in finitely many variables should be
equivalent to a finite subsystem. The question is not a finiteness
assertion about the value set of one fixed word.

Iker de las Heras and Andoni Zozaya, *Strong conciseness and equationally
Noetherian groups*, https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.07427, Theorem1.1, assumes a
profinite group has a DENSE equationally Noetherian subgroup. It concludes
that a word-value set of cardinality less than the continuum is finite.
Corollary1.3 obtains strong conciseness for pro-C completions of residually
C linear groups, including free pro-p groups. It does not assert that the
completion itself is equationally Noetherian. Density cannot simply be
substituted for the missing hypothesis.

The paragraph following Corollary1.2 says that linearity of nonabelian
free pro-p groups over any field is unknown, citing Lubotzky--Shalev1994.
A later sentence about unknown equational Noetherianity refers to
virtually abelian-by-polycyclic groups, not to free pro-p groups. Neither
sentence is treated here as an exact current-status theorem for E6.
