# F28: elliptic rational conjugation lead

28 September 2026, approximately 11:02 UTC (clock checked at 11:03:06 UTC immediately afterwards). This derivation precedes a targeted literature search for this construction. It uses no Kourovka argument or code. Status: candidate route; exact source/render and novelty still to check.

Realize the rank-two free group as the level-two principal congruence subgroup of PSL(2,Z), generated by

    A = [[1,2],[0,1]], B = [[1,0],[2,1]].

Take D=diag(2,1), U=[[0,-1],[1,1]], Q=UD=[[0,-1],[2,1]]. Conjugation f(X)=QXQ^-1 is defined into this free group on the index-two subgroup S whose lower-left entry is divisible by 4. A Schreier basis is A, BAB^-1, B^2. Direct calculations predict

    f(A) = B^-2,
    f(BAB^-1) = B^-1 A^2 B,
    f(B^2) = B^-1 A       (the last equality is in PSL).

Since conjugation is injective, its image is an isomorphic copy of S in F2. It appears to be another subgroup of index two.

Suppose H<=S and f(H)<=H. Every h in H has all iterates Q^n h Q^-n represented by integral determinant-one matrices (signs do not matter). Q has eigenvalues (1 +/- i sqrt(7))/2 of absolute value sqrt(2), so conjugation by its powers is bounded over the reals. Thus the integral orbit of h is finite. Two iterates coincide, and h commutes with Q^k for some k>0.

No positive power of Q is scalar: the ratio of its eigenvalues has trace over Q equal to -3/2, so cannot be a root of unity (a rational algebraic integer must be integral). Consequently Q^k has distinct eigenvalues and its centralizer equals that of Q.

An integral matrix commuting with Q has shape [[a,b],[-2b,a-b]]. Its determinant is a^2-ab+2b^2=(a-b/2)^2+7b^2/4. Determinant one forces b=0 and a=+/-1. It is the identity in PSL. Therefore H is trivial.

An alternative p-adic argument exists but is unnecessary: the two 2-adic eigenvalues of Q have valuations zero and one, so integrality of every forward conjugate forces preservation of an irrational eigenline, hence commutation with Q.
