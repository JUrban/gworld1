## N4

The original asks about the number of isomorphism types among iterated
automorphism groups of any finitely generated torsion-free nilpotent
group. Its background cites Hamkins's transfinite termination theorem;
that is not finite termination.

[Kassabov's 2003 thesis](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0311488) proves finite
termination for free nilpotent groups. Its introduction, printed pp.iv--vi,
distinguishes the general nilpotent conjecture and natural-map versus
isomorphism-type stabilization. [Tolstykh's paper, version 2 from
2009](https://arxiv.org/abs/0807.4341v2), introduction pp.1--2, extends
free-nilpotent results to infinite rank. These are prior results for a
proper subclass, not a full N4 answer. Both PDFs were archived; the
introductions were read, not the complete proofs. No general new route
was obtained. The present bounded search does not establish that the
whole original question remains open today.

## MA6

For nonzero rational a the two printed matrices are simultaneously
conjugate, by diag(1/a,1), to the standard pair with parameters 1 and
alpha=a^2. Thus the intended rank-two freeness question concerns rational
squares in (0,4). The value a=0 produces the trivial group and is not
counted as a solution to the intended question.

[Choi--Jo--Kim--Lee (2024)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.11378), introduction
pp.1--5 and Theorems A--C, supplies finite tests at each specified minimal
relation degree. It does not supply a stopping bound proving freeness
after finitely many unsuccessful degrees.

The newer [Jang--Yi revision of 31 July 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.20524v3),
pp.1--4, still states rational non-freeness as Conjecture 1.2. It proves
that the converse of the orbit test fails and constructs sequences of
non-free rationals approaching 3. A failed orbit test therefore cannot
certify freeness. Its normalization agrees with alpha=a^2 above. The
2024 introduction's absence of sequences approaching 3 is outdated.
Only these statements and introductory explanations were read; neither
paper's entire proof was audited. No new MA6 result follows.
