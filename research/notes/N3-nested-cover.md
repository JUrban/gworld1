# N3: torsion-free covers along a countable chain of nilpotent subgroups

29 September 2026. **Restricted construction, not a solution of N3 and
not an additional candidate count.** The countable case is prior work;
the proof below is recorded to distinguish a valid nested construction
from the false unrestricted profile assertion audited earlier.

## Scope and prior work

The frozen question asks whether every locally nilpotent group is an
image of a torsion-free locally nilpotent group, without a cardinality
restriction. The original statement and rendering are retained in
`problems/N3/` and `research/statement-audits/N3/`.

The Kourovka Notebook, eighteenth edition, problem 3.47, printed p.11,
credits the affirmative countable case to N.S. Romanovskii, Preprint
1969, and the periodic case to E.M. Levich and A.I. Tokarenko (1970).
The [editors' deposited PDF](https://eprints.maths.manchester.ac.uk/2087/1/18tkt.pdf)
is archived as `literature/raw/N3-Kourovka18-status.*`. These original
1969/1970 proofs have not been obtained or re-audited here. Attempts to
retrieve the twentieth-edition PDF at alglog.org failed because of an
expired TLS certificate; the HTTP URL redirected to the same failure.

Göbel–Shelah–Wallutis (2003), Corollary 3.7, asserts the unrestricted
answer. Its torsion-freeness lemma has the explicit counterexample in
[the earlier audit](N3-published-cover-audit.md). Nothing here disproves
that corollary, repairs its arbitrary-cardinality proof, or establishes
novelty of the restricted result.

**Proposition.** Suppose G is an ascending union G=union_{n>=1} G_n,
where every G_n is nilpotent. The G_n need not be finitely generated or
countable. Then G is an image of a torsion-free locally nilpotent group H.
Moreover H can be chosen as an ascending union of nilpotent groups H_n
whose lower central factors are free abelian; each H_n is a retract of
H_(n+1), and the maps H_n -> G_n are compatible epimorphisms.

In particular this recovers the already known countable case by taking
G_n generated by the first n elements. It does not show that every
locally nilpotent group has such a countable chain.

## The integral free-product lemma

Write L(A)=direct_sum_{i>=1} gamma_i(A)/gamma_(i+1)(A), with its usual
graded Lie bracket, and write Lie_Z(X) for the free Lie ring on a set X
of degree-one generators. All tensor products in this note are over Z.

**Lemma.** If every lower central factor of A is free abelian, then

    L(A * F(X)) = L(A) * Lie_Z(X)                              (1)

naturally, where the right-hand product is the coproduct of graded Lie
rings. Its homogeneous components are free abelian. Consequently every
nilpotent quotient (A*F(X))/gamma_(c+1)(A*F(X)) has free abelian lower
central factors, and is torsion-free.

Here F(X) may have arbitrary rank. Mere torsion-freeness of A would not
suffice; the explicit negative control below illustrates this.

### 1. The augmentation associated graded of A

Let I(A) be the augmentation ideal of Z[A] and U(L) the integral
universal enveloping ring. The natural graded ring epimorphism

    theta_A : U(L(A)) -> gr_I Z[A]

sends the class of a in gamma_i/gamma_(i+1) to a-1 modulo I^(i+1).
Its rationalization is an isomorphism by Quillen's group-ring theorem.
Since L(A) is free over Z, the integral PBW theorem makes U(L(A)) free
over Z. The kernel of theta_A is therefore both torsion-free and
rationally zero, hence zero. In particular all augmentation quotients
are free abelian.

The exact integral/rational assertions used here are stated at the
beginning of Section 5, p.24, of M. Hartl,
[*On Fox quotients of arbitrary group algebras*](https://arxiv.org/abs/0707.0281),
arXiv:0707.0281v2 (2010). Hartl explicitly credits the lower-central
case to D. Quillen, *J. Algebra* 10 (1968), 411–418. Hartl's statement
was read and its actual PDF page viewed; the 1968 original was not
obtained. Integral PBW and the Hall basis for a free Lie ring are
classical imported theorems, not new results of this experiment.

### 2. Augmentation ideals in a free product

Put B=F(X), J=I(B). The augmented ring Z[A*B] is the coproduct of
Z[A] and Z[B]. As an abelian group it has the direct-sum normal form

    Z plus all nonempty alternating tensor products of I(A) and J.

In this description the nth augmentation power is exactly the sum of
alternating tensors whose factors have total augmentation degree at
least n. Indeed a product of n augmentation elements has this form
after multiplying adjacent factors from the same ring; conversely a
factor from I(A)^d or J^d is a sum of d-fold products and gives the
reverse inclusion. Adjacent multiplication cannot introduce a scalar
term because both factors lie in augmentation ideals.

The associated graded of each such tensor is the tensor of its
associated gradeds. One can check this in each bounded degree by
splitting the finite augmentation filtrations: their successive
quotients are free abelian. No splitting of an infinite completed
filtration is required. The direct sum over alternating tensors then
gives a natural graded ring isomorphism

    gr_I Z[A*B] = (gr_I Z[A]) * (gr_I Z[B])
                 = U(L(A)) * T_Z(X)
                 = U(L(A) * Lie_Z(X)).                       (2)

The last equality follows from the universal property of enveloping
rings. This argument includes infinite generating sets because every
tensor word has finite length and every degree calculation is finite
in filtration length; the free modules themselves may have infinite rank.

### 3. Freeness of the Lie coproduct

For any Lie ring L free as a Z-module, set M=U(L) tensor Z[X]. Let L
act on M by left multiplication in U(L), and extend the action by
derivations to the free Lie ring Lie_Z(M). Then

    L * Lie_Z(X) = L semidirect Lie_Z(M).                     (3)

For completeness, a map L -> D and arbitrary chosen images of X in
a Lie ring D extend uniquely to the right side. On M the extension
sends u tensor x to the action of u by iterated adjoints on the chosen
image of x. This is L-equivariant, extends uniquely from M to its free
Lie ring, and respects the semidirect bracket. Conversely the right
side is generated by L and the elements 1 tensor x, since U(L) is
generated by L. This is the coproduct universal property.

By PBW, U(L) is free over Z. Thus M is free, its free Lie ring is
free over Z by the Hall basis, and (3) is free over Z. The assertion
respects the grading: give u tensor x degree deg(u)+1 and choose
homogeneous bases.

### 4. Recovering the group Lie ring

Let K=L(A)*Lie_Z(X) and G=A*F(X). The natural map K -> L(G) is onto:
the lower-central graded Lie ring of any group is generated by degree
one, and the degree-one images of A and F(X) generate G_ab.
The composite

    K -> L(G) -> gr_I Z[G] = U(K)

is the canonical PBW map. It is injective because K is Z-free by (3).
Hence K -> L(G) is also injective, proving (1). Passing to G/gamma_(c+1)
keeps precisely the degrees <=c. A nilpotent group with torsion-free
lower central factors is torsion-free, by induction down its finite
central series. This proves the lemma.

## Construction of the cover

Choose increasing positive integers c_n bounding the nilpotency classes
of G_n. Start with H_0=1 and its trivial map. For each n>=1 choose a
set X_n and a map X_n -> G_n whose image, together with G_(n-1),
generates G_n. There is no cardinality requirement on X_n. Define

    H_n = (H_(n-1) * F(X_n)) / gamma_(c_n+1)(H_(n-1) * F(X_n)).

The lemma proves inductively that H_n is torsion-free nilpotent and
has free abelian lower central factors. The retraction killing X_n
factors through H_n because H_(n-1) has class <=c_(n-1)<=c_n.
It is a left inverse to the natural inclusion of H_(n-1), so this
inclusion is injective. The epimorphism of the free product to G_n
also factors through H_n, since G_n has class <=c_n. Its restriction
to H_(n-1) is the preceding map followed by inclusion in G_n.

Let H be the union of the embedded H_n. A torsion element would lie
in a torsion-free H_n. A finite subset lies in some nilpotent H_n.
Thus H is torsion-free and locally nilpotent. The compatible maps
induce an epimorphism H -> G, proving the proposition.

For countably generated G, the same construction can use one new
generator at a time. Its finite stages have the presentation

    F(x_1,...,x_n) / normal_closure{
        gamma_(c_i+1)(F(x_1,...,x_i)) : 1<=i<=n }.

The constraints are nested. This is not the earlier false assertion
for arbitrary overlapping finite subsets of generators.

## Why the torsion-free hypothesis alone fails

Let A be the torsion-free class-two integer-matrix group generated by
a=I+2E12, b=I+2E23, z=I+E13. Thus [a,b]=z^4 and z is central, with
commutator convention [a,b]=a^-1 b^-1 a b. Its abelianization is
Z^2 direct_sum Z/4. In the class-two nilpotent product of A and an
infinite cyclic group <t>, the mixed commutator [z,t] has order four:
the mixed central subgroup is A_ab tensor Z and the class of z has
order four there. This also follows from the explicit class-two
presentation checked in GAP below. Hence the weaker proposed lemma
would be false even with one new free generator.

The same obstruction, using A with itself, appears in Example 3.8 of
J. Brude and R. Sasyk,
[*Permanence properties of verbal products and verbal wreath products of groups*](https://ems.press/content/serial-article-files/30105),
*Groups Geom. Dyn.* 16 (2022), 363–401. The authors credit their matrix
example to their reference [11], Section 1.2.2. Their convention for
group commutators is the other usual convention; it does not change
the stated torsion and abelianization conclusions. Relevant paragraphs
and the actual printed p.378 were read/viewed. No originality is
claimed for this obstruction.

## Limits

The unrestricted question allows groups that have not been supplied
with a countable ascending nilpotent exhaustion. A transfinite version
of this construction has an unresolved step: at a limit ordinal the
union can be locally nilpotent without any global finite nilpotency
class, so the next bounded quotient need not preserve that union or
the target map. Nor have we proved a way to choose mutually compatible
covers over all finite subsets. The arbitrary-cardinality problem
therefore remains unresolved here.

This is supplementary construction and audit work only. The countable
case is explicitly prior; no novelty is established for the slightly
more general exhaustion hypothesis. Counts remain seven whole-entry
candidates, three partial candidates, zero established novel results.

## Finite checks and reproducibility

`scripts/n3_nested_ranks.py` predicts the integral lower-central ranks
using PBW and the free-product Hilbert-series identity

    1/H_(U(L*Lie(X)))(t) = 1/H_(U(L))(t) - |X|t.

It extracts ranks by the Euler transform with exact integer arithmetic,
truncates at the current nilpotency class, and repeats for the next
stage. Free rank-three class-five and constant-class cases provide
closed-form controls. The predictions and fixtures are under
`research/certificates/N3-nested-cover/`.

The separate `scripts/check_n3_nested_gap.g` constructs the groups from
ordinary group commutator relators, using GAP/nq. It checks all integral
abelian invariants of the lower central quotients against the predictions,
checks the torsion subgroup, and for every later stage checks the
preceding generated subgroup and the coordinate retraction. There are
13 stages in five chains, ranks up to four and classes up to five.
The largest predicted rank vector is (4,5,16,35,110). It also independently
checks that the matrix-type factor in the negative control is torsion-free,
that z has infinite order, and that [z,t] has order four in the product.

Recorded runs:

- `n3-nested-ranks-v1`: 0.070 seconds, one core / 4 GB, passed.
- `n3-nested-gap-v1`: 74.015 seconds, one core / 8 GB, passed all
  assertions. Stderr contains two parser warnings about variables inside
  top-level anonymous functions, no runtime error. For later stages its
  printed rank vector is that of the preceding subgroup (the last checked
  object); both the full group and subgroup were correctly asserted.
  The exact executed source is retained in that run directory.

After that pass, the current GAP source replaces the two anonymous
functions by equivalent explicit loops and prints the checked current
stage's vector. No mathematical construction, fixture or assertion
changed. These reporting-only edits were not grounds for another
mathematical run. There were no failed mathematical jobs in this batch.
The runs were sequential; no random choices were made.

The sources, source metadata, viewed pages, proof, scripts and run logs
are hashed in `research/certificates/N3-nested-cover/manifest.json`.
Finite checks support the constructions and expose the weaker false
