## AUX3(b)

Aglaia Myropolska,
[*Andrews-Curtis and Nielsen equivalence relations on some infinite groups*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1304.2668),
Theorem 1.3 and its Grigorchuk corollary give a positive answer. The full
primary PDF is archived; the exact statements and their proof were read.

The theorem covers every finitely generated group whose maximal subgroups
are normal, for every tuple length at least its normal rank. It identifies
AC equivalence with equivalence in the abelianization. Grigorchuk's group
belongs to this class and has both normal and ordinary rank three. Thus
the tuple-length threshold in the website question is covered exactly.
No answer to AUX3(a) is inferred.

## FP17

Abderezak Ould Houcine,
[*Embeddings in finitely presented groups which preserve the center*](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jalgebra.2006.07.015),
Journal of Algebra 307 (2007), 1–23, explicitly reports the positive answer
in the publisher's abstract. A single finitely presented group with
solvable word problem has a centre containing copies of every countable
abelian group, which is stronger than the requested scope.

Evidence level: the primary publisher's indexed abstract was read; the
full proof was not obtained or audited. Direct publisher access returned
HTTP 403, and the old author PDF URL returned 404. The access limitations
are recorded in `literature/FP17-primary-notice.json`. No claim that the
full paper is included in this repository is made.
