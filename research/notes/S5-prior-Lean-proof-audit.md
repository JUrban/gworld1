## Proof structure and reading limits

The proof bounds the number of nonabelian component paths of a
surjective endomorphism using a finite weight extracted from the
abelianization of a group modulo its centre. A factorial iterate
centralizes the off-diagonal components. Integral corrections through
the actual centres make the diagonal maps surjective after a further
iterate. Hopficity of the factors then supplies injectivity on their
derived subgroups; the kernel of the original surjection lies in those
subgroups and vanishes.

The README and the exact statement/final proof modules were read,
together with the finite-path, eventual-centrality, centre-quotient,
integral-correction, congruence-period and weight arguments. Not every
line of every intermediate module was manually audited. The build and
transitive axiom check supply the formal evidence for those imported
steps; no separate informal proof is claimed as our own work.
