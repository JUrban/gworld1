# F1(b) and F26: prior rank-three scope

Recorded 28 September 2026. These are prior results and consequences;
they must not be counted as new solutions from this experiment.

The frozen F26 asks whether the intersection of two automorphism fixed
subgroups in F_n is itself fixed by one automorphism. A. Martino,
*Intersections of automorphism fixed subgroups in the free group of rank
three*, Algebraic & Geometric Topology 4 (2004), 177--198, Corollary 5.3,
answers yes in ambient rank three, even for arbitrary families. Corollary
3.17 gives the same conclusion in every finite rank for a UPG subgroup of
Aut(F_n). This hypothesis concerns the whole generated subgroup; merely
assuming each of two specified generators is UPG does not justify it.

E. Ventura, *Computing fixed closures in free groups*, Illinois J. Math.
54(1), 175--186 (volume dated 2010; copyright 2011), Theorem 9 and
Corollary 10, decide whether a finitely generated H is an intersection of
automorphism fixed subgroups. Combined with Martino's rank-three theorem,
this decides F1(b) for F3. A requested witnessing automorphism can then
be found by enumerating automorphisms, computing their fixed subgroups
and comparing with H. Existence guarantees termination after a yes answer.
Rank two is already credited to Lee's more specific algorithm.

Ventura's all-rank auto-fixed recognition must not be mistaken for the
all-rank single-automorphism recognition requested in F1(b). Section 4
separates those questions. Its introductory rank-two-only description of
known intersection results is not a reason to overlook Martino 2004.
The maximal-rank and free-factor statements mentioned in Ventura's
introduction are additional prior evidence; their original proofs have
not been separately read during this checkpoint.
