Use the notation H(b,C) from `N3-profile-repair-obstruction.md`: three
generators, each coordinate pair of nilpotency class at most b, and the
whole group of class at most C. The earlier pair-bound-four cases were
never reached by the probe that timed out at H(3,8). This separate test
therefore examines new source groups, H(4,7) and H(4,8).

The aim was to look first for torsion in the *last* lower-central layer,
then map any such torsion to H(2,C). Neither final layer has torsion:

Consequently neither target group nor an induced torsion image was
constructed. These results do not say that all torsion in the source
vanishes, that a torsion-free quotient maps onto the target, or that
increasing every profile value by two works. They give no general cover.
No independent second implementation of these new negative probes is
claimed; the earlier positive torsion certificates remain separately
verified by integer Magnus arithmetic and GAP.

The defining relators are all left-normed commutators of length b+1 on
each coordinate pair, starting with [x_j,x_i]. They normally generate
the pair's gamma_(b+1): gamma_2 of a two-generator free group is normally
generated by its basic commutator, and taking successive conjugation
differences generates subsequent lower-central terms. GAP/nq computes
the class-C quotient. All calculations are integral and deterministic.
