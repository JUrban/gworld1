# N8: the class-seven correction kernel in all ranks

Derived 28 September 2026 at approximately 14:52–14:54 UTC. Not yet
counted; this records the argument before higher-rank implementation checks.

Put E=L2 and delta=ad_z, z nonzero in L1, over Q. The derived free Lie
algebra has homogeneous free generators in degrees >=2. In degrees
2,3 these are all of E,L3, and in degree4 a complement W4 to [E,E].
Consequently L''5=[E,L3]=E tensor L3, and

    L''6 = (E tensor W4) direct-sum Lambda^2 L3 direct-sum Lie_3(E).

Here [E,L4] is the first plus third summand. Thus [L3,L3] and [E,L4]
intersect trivially in every rank, extending the earlier explicit rank-two
minor. This gives exactly the Nielsen kernel for the (2,3) first correction.

For the (1,4) correction [U,D]+[z,V]=0, the metabelian module forces
V into L''5, so write it as a tensor in E tensor L3. Its delta image
projected to Lambda^2 L3 is the alternating image of (delta tensor id)V.
Since delta:E->L3 is injective, vanishing means V comes from a symmetric
tensor S in E tensor E under id tensor delta. The remaining equation,
modulo Lie_3(E), is

    U tensor Dbar + (id tensor delta^2) S = 0

in E tensor (L4/[E,E]). The map delta^2:E->L4/[E,E] is injective:
apply the metabelian module, where it multiplies the injective image
of E by the square of a nonzero linear polynomial.

For a nonzero kernel vector U is nonzero, hence Dbar=delta^2 Tbar for
a unique nonzero T in E, and S=-U tensor T. Symmetry implies U is
proportional to T. Write D=delta^2 T+D0 with D0 in [E,E]. The full
equation then forces [T,D0]=0, so D0=0 by the generator-centralizer
argument inside the free Lie algebra generated by E.

Thus the kernel is zero unless D=delta^2 T for a nonzero T in E;
in that case it is the line generated by (T,-[T,delta T]). This is
an all-rank proof, not an inference from the earlier samples.
