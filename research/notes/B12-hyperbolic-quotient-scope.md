# B12: a recent four-strand theorem does not settle higher strands

29 September 2026. No new answer is claimed.

The original B12 paragraph asks about non-elementary hyperbolic quotients
of B_n for n>4. Its rendered statement was inspected. A recent search
found Mangioni--Sisto, *Cusped spaces for hierarchically hyperbolic groups,
and applications to Dehn filling quotients*,
[arXiv:2602.23275](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.23275).

The archived author-hosted PDF has SHA-256
`38393e32f096c682f1ef608e6b6e858727a237fae99211316da76194621d5bbd`.
Read its introduction, particularly Theorem B and Remark F; viewed page
2. Theorem B constructs a non-elementary hyperbolic quotient Q of B_4
for each n>=5 such that every homomorphism B_n -> Q has virtually cyclic
image. This distinguishes some four-strand quotients from higher-strand
quotients. It neither constructs a non-elementary hyperbolic quotient of
B_n for n>=5 nor rules out all possible such quotients.

The statement that a finite-index subgroup of B_n maps onto B_4 also
does not give the required quotient of the whole B_n. The long
hierarchical-hyperbolicity proof was not independently audited. Keep the
actual higher-strand question unresolved here; do not promote a broad
abstract or search summary into a positive answer to B12.
