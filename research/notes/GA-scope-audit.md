# Group-action scope audit

28 September 2026, approximately 14:20–14:43 UTC. Read the complete
original `sources/raw/probact.html` and inspect the GA2/GA3 paragraph
renderings. No new candidate solution results from this pass.

Archived [Kharlampovich–Vdovina, *Beyond Serre's “Trees” in two
directions: Lambda-trees and products of trees* (2017)](https://arxiv.org/abs/1710.10306),
SHA-256 `4a0c6f9469b0b359ad9ba19ca486798f2bf8c64681e706a454ec81634d4799aa`.
Read the introduction, sections 2.3 and 3.2, and section 6. The archived
PDF and searchable text are in `literature/raw/GA-Kharlampovich-Vdovina-1710.10306.*`.

**GA2.** The original asks about finitely **generated** groups acting
freely on a Lambda-tree. Corollary 3 of the survey says finitely
**presented** Lambda-free groups are biautomatic. Section 6, Conjecture 1,
retains finite presentability of every finitely generated Lambda-free
group as a conjecture. Thus the cited theorem covers an important
subclass but does not itself settle the original quantifier. Current
targeted searches found the same distinction in primary ICM2014 and
2013 talk texts; no matching theorem closing the gap was located.
This is a scope check, not a claim that no later result exists.

**GA3.** The literal website statement omits a nonabelian hypothesis.
For G=Z, the commutator of nontrivial elements from the two copies in
G*G is nontrivial but maps trivially under every homomorphism to G.
This elementary omission is recorded and is **not counted as a new
solution** of the intended nonabelian question. Section 2.3 of the
survey gives torsion-freeness, CSA, and the free-or-free-abelian
two-generator subgroup property. These facts alone do not prove that
G discriminates its free square.

In particular, a proposed big-powers argument needs the actual
big-powers hypothesis. The primary texts
[Kharlampovich–Myasnikov, *Implicit function theorem over free groups*, section 2.5](https://web.stevens.edu/algebraic/alexeim/Publications/All_files_temp/implicit_last_78.pdf)
and [*Discriminating and co-discriminating groups*](https://web.stevens.edu/algebraic/alexeim/Publications/All_files_new/dcod_46.pdf)
were checked for this distinction. They were consulted online and are
not represented as archived PDFs here. Merely being CSA does not supply
the needed free-square theorem. The Kvaschuk–Myasnikov–Serbin pregroup
criterion likewise has extra axioms; no argument verifying them for
every group in GA3 was obtained.

**Other boundaries.** An affine action on a Lambda-tree is not the
isometric free action requested by GA1. A finite-presentability or
algorithmic theorem for one Lambda-free subclass does not decide the
elementary theory of the whole class in GA4. No new claim is made
for either entry.
