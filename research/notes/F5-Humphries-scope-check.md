# F5: scope of Humphries's representation construction

29 September 2026. No new solution or count change.

The complete four-page [Humphries preprint](https://shpilrain.ccny.cuny.edu/gworld/problems/Aut_F3.pdf)
was read, including its Magma listing; that computation was not rerun.
The abstract and conclusion on page 3 concern Aut(F_3), with infinitely
many irreducible representations in dimensions at most 42. One introductory
sentence instead writes Aut(F_n), and appears in search snippets. The
construction and appendix supply no general-rank extension; that sentence
is not evidence for higher ranks. Page 3 was actually rendered and viewed.
The frozen background already credits the rank-two and rank-three results.

Nitsche's [primary abstract](https://arxiv.org/abs/2009.05134v3) proves
property (T) for Aut(F_4). As an inference using standard permanence of
property (T), every finite-index subgroup has finite abelianization.
Thus inducing a variable one-dimensional character from a finite-index
subgroup cannot give the same construction in rank four. This does not
establish rigidity for all complex irreducible representations. Only
Nitsche's abstract was checked; its certificate was not replayed.
