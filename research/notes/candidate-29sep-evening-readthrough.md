The main points checked were:

- N8's rational homogeneous changes of coordinates do not replace the
  integral correction lattices. The tail subalgebras actually contain
  the coefficients to which the free-basis theorem is applied. In the
  first-exception block the strict inequalities p>0 and q>p+t keep
  quadratic changes of the logarithmic coordinates and parameter-dependent
  two-Y terms above the required degree. The diagonal argument kills
  all later compatible columns, not just isolated homogeneous kernels.
- N5 retains the finite kernel after quotienting by the actual centre.
  Rational partitions do not replace the bounded-index subgroup search.
  A primitive exact sum in the centre is necessary, and the finite
  congruence search uses reductions of actual integral automorphisms.
- F28's bounded integral conjugation orbit and the all-exponent recurrence
  apply to every element of any invariant subgroup, including infinitely
  generated subgroups. No normality assumption is inserted.
- GA3 chooses the collapse scale from translation lengths, and its
  final factor maps extend to the whole group. The general-type boundary
  argument uses the imported tree facts and does not follow from CSA alone.
- F41's separating formula is parameter-free, while its eventual
  multipattern may have fixed coefficients. The finite-translate generic
  side is the complement of the orbit-containing set. The polynomial
  counting constants depend on the individual word.

No new gap was identified at these points. This does not independently
establish the imported model-theoretic, Lie-theoretic or tree-action
