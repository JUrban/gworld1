## Recovery and the effective bound

sets. A strict bridge has no horizontal edge at its starting height,
whereas terminal horizontal edges belong to the preceding band.
Positive height difference makes each piece's boundary nonzero and
recovers its endpoint. Restriction to bands and normalization therefore
recover each factor element from the product flow, even with cancelled
excursions and repeated edges.

The last occurrence of each alternating extremum gives strictly
decreasing positive spans. A nonempty remaining piece starts by leaving

representatives deterministically supplies the required injection on

with P(t) at most exp(2 sqrt(t)).
