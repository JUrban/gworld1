STALLINGS EQUALIZER CONJECTURE
JIALIN LEI AND TENG ZHANG
Dedicated to the memory of Professor J. R. Stallings
Abstract.The famous Stallings equalizer conjecture has remained open for more than 40
years, which asserts that for any free groupFn of rankn≥2, any free groupF, and any two
monomorphismsg, h:F n→F,the equalizerEq(g, h) ={w∈F n|g(w) =h(w)}satisfies
rk Eq(g, h)≤n.The only known case isn= 2, due to A. D. Logan in 2022. By introducing
the notion of colored Stallings graphs, we show that for every integern≥2there exist
monomorphismsg, h:F n−→F 2 such thatrk Eq(g, h)≥2n−2.This disproves the Stallings
equalizer conjecture forn≥3.

Conjecture 1.1(Stallings).Letn≥2. LetF n be a free group of rankn, and letFbe a free
group. Letg,h:F n→Fbe monomorphisms. Then
rk Eq(g,h)≤n.
Goldstein and Turner [7] proved that under the hypotheses of Conjecture 1.1,Eq(g,h)is
finitely generated, that is,Eq(g,h)has finite rank. Later, they [8] proved that under the weaker
assumption that at least one ofgandhis injective,Eq(g,h)has finite rank. Conjecture 1.1 is
also considered under such weaker assumption, see [1, Problem F31], [6, Problem 6] and [16,
Conjecture 8.3].
In the special case whereΣ = ∆andh= id, the equalizer is precisely the fixed subgroup.
Indeed, for any endomorphismϕ:F(Σ)→F(Σ),
Fix(ϕ) ={w∈F(Σ)|ϕ(w) =w}= Eq(ϕ,id).
Thus the fixed subgroup problem is the one-map special case of the equalizer problem. By
using train-track maps, Bestvina and Handel [3] proved the well-known Scott conjecture for
automorphisms, that is, Conjecture 1.1 holds in the special case whereFn =F,g∈Aut(F n)
andh= id. Later, Imrich and Turner [9] extended Bestvina–Handel’s results to all endo-
morphisms. Dicks and Ventura [6] proved stronger inertness results for families of injective
endomorphisms, and Bergman [2] extended their results to families of endomorphisms. For
related recent developments, see [10, 12].
Forgeneralequalizers, thesituationisharder. Ventura[16]notedthattheinjectivityhypoth-
esis in Conjecture 1.1 is essential: without it, equalizers may have infinite rank. Ciobanu and
Logan [5] proved algorithmic and rank results for immersions, relating the problem to the Post
Correspondence Problem. Logan [13] proved the casen= 2in Conjecture 1.1 and obtained
further positive results under inertness or retract hypotheses on the images. Very recently,
Carvalho and Silva [4] investigated the fixed points and equalizers of injective endomorphisms
of the free group at infinity.
The purpose of this paper is to give a family of explicit counterexamples to Conjecture 1.1
forn≥3. Our main result is the following.
Theorem 1.2.Letn≥2andF n be a free group of rankn. Then there exist monomorphisms
g,h:F n−→F 2 such that
rk Eq(g,h)≥2n−2.
Consequently, Conjecture 1.1 is false for everyn≥3.
Remark 1.3.In fact, we construct a subgroup familyAn of rank2n−2such that
An≤ff Eq(g,h),
where≤ ff denotes a free factor inclusion. In particular,
rk Eq(g,h)≥2n−2.
Moreover, the same family shows that the analogue of Conjecture 1.1 is false if one replaces
the assumption that bothgandhare injective by the assumption thatgis injective andhis
not, for everyn≥5; see Proposition 5.1.
