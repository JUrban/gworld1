                        THE IDENTITIES OF A GROUP
                  WITH NILPOTENT COMMUTATOR SUBGROUP
                            ARE FINITELY BASED
UDC 512.543.2

                                         A. N. KRASIL'NIKOV


         ABSTRACT. It is shown that the identities of a group with nilpotent commutator sub-
         group are finitely based. It follows that the identities of a connected matrix group over
         a field are finitely based.

                                             Introduction
    Soon after the finite basis problem for group identities was solved in the negative
by Ol'shanskii [1], Adyan [2] and Vaughan-Lee [3], the following question was posed
 [3]. Does every group with nilpotent commutator subgroup have a finite basis for its
 identities? The importance of these groups stems mainly from Platonov's observation
 [4] that every matrix group over a field that satisfies a nontrivial identity is soluble-
by-finite, and thus, by the Kolchin-Mal'tsev theorem, is a finite extension of a group
with nilpotent commutator subgroup.
    Cohen [5] proved that the identities of every metabelian group (group with abelian
commutator subgroup) are finitely based, using the techniques of Higman [6]. Co-
hen's result has been strengthened successively in articles of Vaughan-Lee [7], McKay
 [8], and Bryant and Newman [9]; finally, it was proved in [9] that the identities of a
group whose commutator subgroup is nilpotent of class 2 are finitely based, as well
 as those of some other groups. The methods of [9] are used in [10] to show that
the identities of every extension of a nilpotent group by an abelian group of finite
 exponent are finitely based; in particular, this is true for supersoluble groups.
    The main result of this paper is as follows.
    THEOREM 1. The identities of every group with nilpotent commutator subgroup are
finitely based.
    Since every Zariski-connected matrix group over a field satisfying a nontrivial
 identity is soluble (see [4]), and every connected soluble linear group has nilpotent
 commutator subgroup, the theorem has the following consequence.
    COROLLARY. The identities of every connected linear group over a field are finitely
 based.
    The method developed in the proof of Theorem 1 can be used in certain cases to
 investigate the finite basis probelm for representations of groups (for the definition
 of identities of a group representation and associated concepts, see [11]-[13]).
