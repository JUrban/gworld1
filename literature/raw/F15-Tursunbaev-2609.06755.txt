Subgroups of free groups with the same commutator subgroup
TURSUNBAEV ARTUR
Abstract. We prove that any two subgroups of a free group with the same nontrivial commuta-
tor subgroup are equal.This yields an affirmative answer to Shpilrain’s Problem F15: normality
of [R, R] in a free groupF implies normality of the noncyclic subgroupR. No restrictions on
ranks or indices are imposed.
1.Introduction
In 1990, V.E. Shpilrain posed, in the 11th edition ofThe Kourovka Notebook, the question of
whether normality of the commutator subgroup of a noncyclic subgroup of a free group implies
normality of the subgroup itself; see [7, Problem 11.124]. This question was subsequently restated
as Problem F15 in the book by Kapovich, Myasnikov, and Shpilrain [6, Problem F15, p. 10].
The motivation comes from the classical Auslander–Lyndon theorem: ifN and M are normal
subgroups of a nonabelian free group andN′≤M ′, thenN≤M . The normality assumption
is essential here: Dunwoody [5, p. 153] constructed subgroupsN,M≤F such thatM ◁F,
N′≤M ′, butN≰M . Thus, the normality assumption onN cannot simply be omitted from
the Auslander–Lyndon theorem.
In this context, Shpilrain’s problem has the following precise formulation: ifF is a noncyclic
free group,R≤Fis a noncyclic subgroup, and
R′◁F,
must we have
R◁F?
In this paper, we answer this question in the affirmative. Throughout, we use the convention
[a,b] =a −1b−1ab, H ′ = [H,H].
Theorem 1.1.LetFbe a free group of arbitrary rank, and letH,K≤F. If
H′ =K′̸= 1,
thenH=K.
Corollary 1.2(F15).LetFbe a free group, and letR≤Fbe a noncyclic subgroup. Then
R′◁F=⇒R◁F.
Derivation of the corollary from the theorem.The subgroupR is free and noncyclic, soR′̸= 1.
For everyf∈F, normality ofR ′ gives
(f−1Rf)′ =f−1R′f=R ′.
Theorem 1.1 impliesf−1Rf=R. HenceR◁F.□
Another immediate consequence of the main theorem is the equality
NF (H′) =N F (H)
for every noncyclic subgroupHof a free groupF.
The main theorem can also be formulated as the assertion that the map

is injective on the set of subgroupsH≤FwithH ′̸= 1.
