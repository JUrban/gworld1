                                                   Abstract. We investigate the palindromic width of finitely generated solvable
                                                   groups. We prove that every finitely generated 3-step solvable group has finite
                                                   palindromic width. More generally, we show the finiteness of palindromic width for
                                                   finitely generated abelian-by-nilpotent-by-nilpotent groups. For arbitrary solvable
                                                   groups of step ≥ 3, we prove that if G is a finitely generated solvable group that is
                                                   an extension of an abelian group by a group satisfying the maximal condition for
                                                   normal subgroups, then the palindromic width of G is finite. We also prove that
                                                   the palindromic width of Z ≀ Z with respect to the set of standard generators is 3.




                                                                                  1. Introduction
                                             Let G be a group with a set of generators X. A reduced word in the alphabet
                                          X ±1 is a palindrome if it reads the same forwards and backwards. The palindromic
                                          length lP (g) of an element g in G is the minimum number k such that g can be
                                          expressed as a product of k palindromes. The palindromic width of G with respect
                                          to X is defined to be pw(G, X) = sup lP (g). When there is no confusion about the
                                                                                    g∈G
                                          underlying generating set X, we simply denote the palindromic width with respect to
                                          X by pw(G). Palindromes in groups have already proved useful in studying various
                                          aspects of combinatorial group theory and geometry, for example see [9]–[13], [15]–[19],
                                          [21].
                                             For g, h in G, the commutator of g and h is defined as [g, h] = g −1 h−1 gh. If C is the
                                          set of commutators in some group G then the commutator subgroup G′ is generated
                                          by C. The commutator length lC (g) of an element g ∈ G′ is the minimal number k
                                          such that g can be expressed as a product of k commutators. The commutator width
                                          of G is defined by sup lC (g) and is denoted by cw(G). It is well known [22] that
                                                                 g∈G
                                          the commutator width of a free non-abelian group is infinite, but the commutator
                                          width of a finitely generated nilpotent group is finite (see [3, 4]). An algorithm of the
                                          computation of the commutator length in free non-abelian groups can be found in [5].

   In [7], we initiated the investigation of finding the palindromic width of a finitely
generated group that is free in some variety of groups. We proved that the palindromic
width of a finitely generated free nilpotent group is finite. Further using a result of
Akhavan-Malayeri and Rhemtulla [2], we proved that pw(Fn (UN ), X) ≤ 5n, where U is
the variety of abelian groups, N is the variety of nilpotent groups and X = {x1 , . . . , xn }
is a basis of the group Fn (UN ) that is free in UN . In our work we also observed that
there is a surprising analogy between commutator widths and palindromic widths in
groups, though we did not have a clear picture about the relationship. We asked [7,
Problem 2] a question to obtain a connection between the two widths. In this paper
we also investigated this question and obtained further results in this direction, see
Section 2.
   The main aim of this paper is to investigate the palindromic width of some solvable
groups and the wreath product of cyclic groups. We found a striking analogy of the
palindromic widths and commutator widths of solvable groups. Rhemtulla [23, Theo-
rem 1] proved that if A is a normal abelian subgroup of a solvable group G such that
G/A satisfies the maximal condition for normal subgroups, then cw(G) < ∞. The
following is an analogous theorem for palindromes.
Theorem 1.1. Let A be a normal abelian subgroup of a finitely generated solvable
group G = hXi such that G/A satisfies the maximal condition for normal subgroups.
Then pw(G, X) < ∞.
   We recall that a group G is said to satisfy the maximal condition for normal sub-
groups if every normal subgroup of G is the normal closure of a finite subset of G. Our
proof of the above theorem is based on the approach of Rhemtulla [23] where he studied
commutator width in finitely generated solvable groups. Essentially, we have been able
to transfer the language of commutator width into that of palindromes. Rhemtulla [23]
also obtained finiteness of the commutator width of free 3-step solvable groups. In this
paper, we obtain analogous results for palindromic width of free 3-step solvable groups.
We further investigated the palindromic width of free abelian-by-nilpotent-by-nilpotent
groups. We prove the following theorem in Section 4.
Theorem 1.2. Let G be a finitely generated group in the variety UN N . Then the
palindromic width of G is finite.
    As a corollary we have:
Corollary 1.3. Every finitely generated 3-step solvable group has finite palindromic
width.
   We do not know whether the palindromic width of an arbitrary finitely generated
solvable group (of step > 3) is finite. Note, that an answer on the similar question on
the commutator width also unknown.
   In [8], we have proved that the palindromic width of a finitely generated free
metabelian group of rank n is at most 4n − 1. It would be interesting to obtain
an exact value for the palindromic width of a finitely generated metabelian group. It is
indeed possible to obtain a precise value of the palindromic widths for some metabelian
groups. In this paper, we consider the special case of the metabelian group G = Z ≀ Z.
If a and b are the generators of the first and the second cyclic group in the above
            PALINDROMIC WIDTH OF FINITELY GENERATED SOLVABLE GROUPS                     3


wreath product, then we prove that pw(Z ≀ Z, {a, b}) = 3, see Section 5. Along the way
we also show that the commutator width of Z ≀ Z is 1.
    After finishing this work, we learned that Riley and Sale [24] have also studied
palindromic width of solvable and metableian groups using different techniques. They
have proved the special case of Theorem 1.1 when A is the trivial subgroup, that
is, their result states that the palindromic width of a finitely generated solvable group
that satisfies the maximal condition for normal subgroups has finite palindromic width.
They have proved this using a result of Akhavan-Malayeri [1]. Riley and Sale have also
proved that the palindromic width of Z≀Z is 3. Their proof is based on their estimate of
pw(G ≀ Z r ), where G is a finitely generated group. On the other hand, our proof relies
on the fact (proved in Section 5) that any element in the commutator subgroup of Z ≀ Z
is a commutator. Palindromic width in wreathe products has also been investigated by
Fink [14] who has also obtained an estimate of pw(G ≀ Zr ). Fink has also obtained the
finiteness of palindromic widths of wreathe products of some more classes of groups.

                                    6. Open Questions
    We proved that for group G in Theorems 1.1 and 1.2 the palindromic width pw(G)
is finite.
Question 1. Let G be a group in Theorems 1.1 and 1.2. Find the lower and upper
bounds on the palindromic width pw(G).
   In the last section we proved that the palindromic width of a solvable Baumslag-
Solitar group is equal 2. For the non solvable groups we can formulate the following.
Question 2. Let
                    BS(m, n) = ha, t || t−1 am t = an i, m, n ∈ Z \ {0}
be a non solvable Baumslag-Solitar group. Is it true that pw(BS(m, n), {a, t}) is infi-
nite?
12               VALERIY G. BARDAKOV AND KRISHNENDU GONGOPADHYAY


   Also, we can formulate the general question on the palindromic width of HNN-
extensions.
Question 3. Let G = hXi be a group and A and B are proper isomorphic subgroups
of G and ϕ : A → B be an isomorphism. Is it true that the HNN-extension
                                  G∗ = hG, t || t−1 At = B, ϕi
of G with associated subgroups A and B has infinite palindromic width with respect to
the generating set X ∪ {t}?
   In [10] palindromic widths of free products were investigated. For the generalized
free products we can formulate:
Question 4. Let G = A ∗C B be a free product of A and B with amalgamated subgroup
C and |A : C| ≥ 3, |B : C| ≥ 2. Is it true that pw(G, {A, B}) is infinite?
  It would be interesting to understand palindromic width of wreath products. In
particular, one can ask the following.
Question 5. Let G = A ≀ B be a wreath product of group A = hXi and B = hY i such
that pw(A, X) < ∞ and pw(B, Y ) < ∞. Is it true that pw(G, X ∪ Y ) < ∞?
   Riley and Sale [24] have proved that if B is a finitely generated abelian group then
the answer is positive. Fink [14] has also proved the finiteness of palindromic width of
wreath product in for some more cases. However, the general case when B is a finitely
generated non-abelian group is still open.
