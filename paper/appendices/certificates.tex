\section{Certificates, implementations and formalization limits}\label{app:certificates}

\subsection{The final growth certificate}\label{app:g9-certificate}
A bridge atom has no internal level at which just one vertical edge
has nonzero flow. Such an edge must have coefficient $+1$ by total
flux. In a product of atoms, exactly these separating cuts mark the
factor boundaries. Restriction to successive edge bands recovers
their flows and endpoints. Thus distinct atoms of arbitrary positive
heights freely generate a monoid. For a specified alphabet with
cost series $F(t)$, its positive root $F(R)=1$ gives
$\lambda_2\ge1/R$; for infinite alphabets exhaust by finite subsets.
Costs are lengths of supplied representatives, not asserted geodesic
lengths.

The initial certificate has 21,483 distinct atoms of costs at most
14, with count vector
\[
 (1,2,2,2,2,6,26,71,162,341,779,1961,5098,13030).
\]
Write $a$ for the vertical and $b$ for the horizontal generator.
For height at least two, the operations
$P_{s,t}(w)=ab^sa^{-1}wb^t$ preserve atom status. The fixed initial
vertical edge cancels as indicated in a bridge representative. Removing
the horizontal boundary lines at heights one and $H$, and normalizing
the remaining flow by its first internal vertical support, classifies
the resulting $\Z^2$ orbits. Equality of normalized cores plus endpoint
coordinates implies equality of full flows: their difference would be
a finitely supported circulation on two disjoint lines, hence zero.
Height one is the separate family $ab^m$.

For a retained orbit with seed coordinates $(x_j,z_j)$ and costs $c_j$,
the cost at $(x,z)\in\Z^2$ is
\[
 f(x,z)=\min_j(c_j+|x-x_j|+|z-z_j|).
\]
Outside the seed bounding rectangle, clamping either coordinate adds
exactly its outside distance. The central rectangle, four strips and
four quadrants therefore give a rational series
$F_{\rm old}(t)=C(t)+S(t)/(1-t)+Q(t)/(1-t)^2$, including the
height-one series. Shortened representatives add 320 seeds to the
original list, with the same full flows and verified smaller costs.
The finite contracted-grid support-connection calculation uses the
credited flow/Euler construction~\cite{mruv}; its polynomial data are
retained in the
\record{research/certificates/G9-steiner-completion-v1/manifest-v1.json}{completion manifest}.

For height $H\ge3$, add the Nielsen shear
$\alpha_k(a)=ab^k$, $\alpha_k(b)=b$. It commutes with both boundary
operations. If $x_1,x_2$ are the minimum horizontal coordinates of
nonzero vertical support at internal levels one and two, and $(H,y)$
is the endpoint, put
\[
 d=x_2-x_1,\quad u=x_1-d,\quad v=y-x_1-(H-1)d.
\]
The boundary action changes $(u,v)$ freely, while $\alpha_k$ changes
$d$ to $d+k$. Applying $\alpha_{-d}$ and $P_{-u,-v}$ and retaining
the \emph{full} normalized flow classifies the free $\Z^3$ action.
In each such orbit the old alphabet occupies finitely many complete
$d$-planes. Add one tail strictly beyond each extremal plane, with
all boundary coordinates. The two tails and all old planes are
disjoint by their coordinates; different normalized flows give
disjoint orbits.

A chosen seed with $V$ vertical letters and initial tail cost $L$
contributes
\[
 \frac{t^L}{1-t^V}\left(\frac{1+t}{1-t}\right)^2.
\]
The 21,803 retained seeds give 1,453 shear orbits and 2,906 tails.
Their aggregated additional series is
\begin{align*}
 F(t)-F_{\rm old}(t)=\left(\frac{1+t}{1-t}\right)^2
 \bigg(&\frac{80t^{19}+236t^{20}+1658t^{21}}{1-t^7}\\
 &+\frac{68t^{22}+448t^{23}}{1-t^9}
 +\frac{6t^{22}+48t^{23}+362t^{24}}{1-t^{10}}\bigg).
\end{align*}
Exact arithmetic verifies
\[
 F(10^9/2676891785)\ge1,\qquad
 F(10^9/2676891786)<1.
\]
Only the lower endpoint of the resulting reciprocal-root interval is
a lower bound on group growth; its upper endpoint is not a group
upper bound. The independent upper certificate lists 648 words
replaceable by shorter or lexicographically smaller equal-flow words.
Every shortlex least representative avoids them. Its 1,968-state
avoidance automaton has adjacency matrix $A$ and an explicit positive
integer vector $v$ satisfying
\[
 Av\le \frac{2943737759}{10^9}v.
\]
This bounds accepted words, hence group balls, at that exponential rate.
Separate GAP checks reconstruct all relations, transitions and integer
inequalities. The
\record{problems/G9/shear-tail-supplement.md}{shear supplement}
and \record{problems/G9/flow-growth-audit.md}{upper-bound audit}
link the exact certificates and commands.

The N8 higher-weight negative control is illustrative. In rank two and
class eleven, the full solver rejects all four normalized weight types;
the two surviving leading branches give quadratics $T^2-2T+2$ and
$T^2+2T+2$. Native GAP reconstructs both integer kernels, 64 actual
group columns and the two polynomials. It does not independently
recompute the projective leading-pair lists. The complete
\record{problems/N8/higher-negative-audit.md}{negative audit} states
that boundary explicitly.

The formal environment was Lean 4.24.0 with pinned Mathlib. The
\record{reports/REPRODUCTION_GUIDE.md}{guide} gives exact revisions.
An axiom audit and \texttt{--trust=0} do not mean every cached Mathlib
artifact was rebuilt or checked by a second kernel. Formalization
claims in this paper are confined to their stated fragments.
