\begin{abstract}
We report a second 48-hour experiment in mathematical research with
OpenAI's GPT-6 Astra, running through Codex with GAP, exact programs,
Lean and access to mathematical literature. At Vladimir Shpilrain's
suggestion, the input was the GroupWorld collection of problems in
combinatorial group theory. Its archived catalogue contains 195 numbered
entries, including problems and subparts already answered in the literature.
The deadline record contains proposed complete coverage of ten entries
and partial results on two others. Complete coverage combines eleven
proposed components with four explicitly credited prior subparts; it is
not a count of established new theorems. The arguments concern virtual
isomorphisms of free groups, nilpotent-group algorithms, automorphic
orbits, free metabelian groups and actions on ordered trees. Partial
results give effective growth approximation and an infinite family of
special braids on five strands. We present the mathematical arguments,
their imported ingredients and the checks performed during the run.
Independent specialist review of correctness and novelty remains
outstanding. The public repository preserves the original statements,
failed attempts, exact certificates and the unchanged deadline assessment.
\end{abstract}
