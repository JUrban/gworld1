Manuscript preparation, 30 September 2026. These checks concern citation
metadata and the stated dependencies. They do not constitute a new
exhaustive literature search or independent verification of every cited proof.
The research-period reading limits remain in `literature/LEDGER.md` and
the per-entry audits at the handoff commit.

- The Kourovka citation is its full version 4, dated 19 September 2026.
  The public commit `dce7931e980c7db83100a07fbe084fc1dd655fdb` and PDF
  retrieval/hash are recorded in `provenance.json`. This is the filtered
  public history, not an assumption that the local parent commit is public.
  The paper and repository are cited separately.
- Gupta–Gupta–Roman'kov, *Primitivity in free groups and free metabelian
  groups*, Canadian Journal of Mathematics 44 (1992), 516–523:
  corrected DOI `10.4153/CJM-1992-033-0`, checked against the publisher's
  primary PDF. Lemma 1 is the imported Jacobian criterion.
- Lee, arXiv:0802.0584: corrected the title to *On several problems about
  automorphisms of the free group of rank two*, using the archived primary
  text. Lee's separate 2006 substitution result and 2007 rank-two
  translation-equivalence algorithm are credited separately.
- Guba, arXiv:math/0508422: corrected the title to *Strict dead end elements
  in free soluble groups*, using the archived primary text.
- Duminil-Copin–Ganguly–Hammond–Manolescu: publication metadata checked
  against the primary arXiv/author record: Annals of Probability 48(4)
  (2020), 1644–1692. The imported Hammersley–Welsh estimate is identified
  by section; the GroupWorld application remains a candidate argument.
- Clark–Goldstein's 2005 stability result and Lee's 2006 substitution
  result are credited from the archived GroupWorld background. The
  bibliography says so; it does not imply a new full-text proof audit.
- Deep dependencies are identified where used: Kharlampovich–Myasnikov
  and Pillay for F41; Guirardel for GA3; Diekert–Elder for the full
  equation-language construction; Sela for graded shortening; published
  nilpotent decomposition and free-Lie results for N5/N8; DPRM and the
  earlier uniform undecidability/retract results for N9; flow and braid
  structure for the partial entries. Exact reading boundaries and related
  prior comparisons are retained in the frozen entry audits.
- Achyuth Jayadevan is named for the reproduced prior A5/S5 Lean work;
  F11/F42 rediscoveries and the Lei–Zhang F31 example are excluded from
  the candidate count. Their detailed references remain in the linked
  literature ledger and scope report.

The automated editorial audit checks that every citation key exists and
that the compiled bibliography and internal references resolve. It does
not decide whether an imported theorem has been applied correctly.
