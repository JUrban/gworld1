# Single commutators in class seven, all finite ranks

Candidate extension, 28 September 2026, first argument approximately
14:52–14:54 UTC. This note supplies the two all-rank kernel statements
needed to extend the earlier rank-two class-seven procedure. Computational
audit and candidate status are recorded separately. No independent
specialist review or exhaustive novelty certification has occurred.

**Proposed theorem.** Given any finite rank r and an element of
`F_r/gamma_8(F_r)`, one can decide whether it is a single commutator
and construct factors when it is. This remains partial scope of N8(b).
Ranks zero and one are immediate. Below r>=2 and rational vector-space
calculations take place in the free Lie algebra L over Q. The actual
group algorithm uses integral Hall coordinates throughout.

## 1. The graded structure used

Write E=L2, B=L3, and choose a vector-space complement W to [E,E] in L4.
The derived subalgebra L' has a homogeneous free generating set whose
degree-two, degree-three and degree-four generators are bases of E, B,
and W respectively. Here is the precise standard ingredient: an
irredundant homogeneous subset of a free Lie algebra freely generates
its subalgebra (the homogeneous form of Shirshov's lemma). Choose
homogeneous lifts of a basis of L'/[L',L']; they generate L' by induction
on weight and are irredundant by projection to this abelianization.
Thus the lemma applies. A primary account states this form explicitly
in Bryant–Kovacs–Stohr, *Subalgebras of free restricted Lie algebras*,
Bull. Austral. Math. Soc. 72 (2005), 147–156, introduction. Only the
ordinary characteristic-zero Lie assertion is used here, not any
restricted-Lie assertion. The archived source is
`literature/raw/N8-Bryant-Kovacs-Stohr-2005.pdf`.

The free generating set and its weights give the following direct
decompositions of the indicated components of L''=[L',L']:

    (L'')5 = [E,B] = E tensor B,
    (L'')6 = [E,W] direct-sum [B,B] direct-sum [E,[E,E]]
            = (E tensor W) direct-sum Lambda^2 B direct-sum Lie_3(E).

These are decompositions by the numbers and types of free generators
in a Lie word: weights 2+3 in degree five, and 2+4, 3+3, 2+2+2 in
degree six. In particular

    [E,L4] intersects [B,B] trivially,
    [E,L4]/[E,[E,E]] = E tensor (L4/[E,E]).              (8)

The displayed identifications include injectivity, not just a
spanning assertion. The algebra generated by E is a free Lie algebra
on E. The elementary generator-centralizer proof from the class-five
note therefore applies inside that algebra as well.

## 2. The degree-five metabelian kernel

Use the polynomial module map mu from the class-five/six proofs.
For nonzero z in L1 put delta=ad_z and write ell_z for its nonzero
linear polynomial. We have mu(delta V)=ell_z mu(V), mu([L',L'])=0,
and mu is injective on E. The earlier degree-four result says
`ker(mu|L4)=[E,E]`.

We also need `ker(mu|L5)=[E,B]`. The image of L5 is precisely the
degree-four polynomial vectors (P_i) satisfying sum t_i P_i=0.
The same elementary syzygy proof in the class-six note applies:
the basic vectors t_i e_j-t_j e_i generate this space, and their
cubic polynomial multiples are images of iterated degree-one
brackets with [X_i,X_j]. Hence the image has dimension

    r*binomial(r+3,4)-binomial(r+4,5).

Subtracting this from Witt's formula dim L5=(r^5-r)/5 yields

    ((r^2-r)/2)*((r^3-r)/3) = dim E * dim B.

By Section 1, [E,B] has exactly this dimension and is contained in
the kernel. It therefore equals the kernel, proving the assertion.

Both delta:E->B and the map

    f:E->L4/[E,E], T->delta^2 T mod [E,E]               (9)

are injective. The first follows by the degree-one centralizer lemma.
For the second, applying mu to delta^2 T gives ell_z^2 mu(T);
the polynomial ring is a domain and mu is injective on E.

## 3. The (2,3) correction kernel in arbitrary rank

For nonzero C in E and D in B consider

    B+L4 -> L6, (U,V)->[U,D]+[C,V].

By (8) its two summands cannot cancel, so each is zero. The exterior
map Lambda^2 B -> L6 is injective by Section 1; hence U is
proportional to D. To prove V=0, first project [C,V]=0 modulo
[E,[E,E]]. Equation (8) gives C tensor Vbar=0, so V belongs to [E,E].
Now [C,V]=0 lies in the free Lie algebra on E. Changing a rational
basis of E makes C one of its free generators, whose centralizer
has no homogeneous degree-two part. Thus V=0.

The rational kernel is exactly Q*(D,0), and its integral kernel is
generated by `(D/content(D),0)`. Exact Nielsen moves x->yx normalize
the resulting affine parameter to finitely many residues, exactly
as in the rank-two procedure.

## 4. Classifying the (1,4) correction kernel

Fix nonzero z in L1 and D in L4. Consider

    E+L5 -> L6, (U,V)->[U,D]+delta V.                  (10)

Its kernel is zero unless D=delta^2 T for some nonzero T in E.
If that equality holds, its kernel is exactly

    Q*(T,-[T,delta T]).                               (11)

**Proof.** For a kernel vector, apply mu. The first bracket vanishes
under mu, so ell_z mu(V)=0 and Section 2 gives V in [E,B]. Identify
V with its unique tensor in E tensor B. The Lambda^2 B component
of delta V in Section 1 is the alternating image of
`(delta tensor id)V`. The term [U,D] has no such component, so
this alternating image is zero.

Since delta:E->B is injective, this implies

    V=(id tensor delta)S

for a symmetric tensor S in E tensor E. To check the linear algebra,
choose B=delta(E) direct-sum B0. The component in delta(E) wedge B0
forces the B0 part of V to be zero. The remaining tensor alternates
to zero exactly when it is symmetric, since the characteristic is zero.
Under the bracket interpretation this means, for any basis (e_i) of E,

    V=sum s_ij [e_i,delta e_j],  s_ij=s_ji.

Differentiation gives a sum of [delta e_i,delta e_j] terms, which
cancels by symmetry, and the sum s_ij[e_i,delta^2 e_j]. Project (10)
modulo [E,[E,E]] and apply (8), obtaining

    U tensor Dbar + (id tensor f)S = 0.               (12)

If U=0, injection of ad_z on L5 gives V=0. Suppose U is nonzero.
Projecting (12) to E tensor coker(f) gives Dbar in im(f). There is a
unique T in E with f(T)=Dbar. Injection of id tensor f then gives
S=-U tensor T. If T=0, S=V=0, D belongs to [E,E], and [U,D]=0
contradicts the generator-centralizer argument in the free algebra on E
unless D=0. The latter is excluded. Hence T is nonzero.

Symmetry of S forces U=kT for a nonzero rational k. Put
D0=D-delta^2 T, so D0 belongs to [E,E]. The full equation, before
projection, becomes k[T,D0]=0 because V=-k[T,delta T]. Again the
centralizer argument in the free algebra on E forces D0=0. Thus
D=delta^2 T and every nonzero kernel vector has the form (11).
Conversely, the derivation identity gives

    delta[T,delta T]=[T,delta^2 T],

so (11) really is a kernel vector. This proves the classification.

This proof uses rational decompositions only to classify the kernel.
For the group decision, the full **integral** kernel and affine
particular solution are computed by integer Smith or Hermite normal form. A rational T
need not have integral Hall coordinates; no integrality is assumed.

## 5. Completing the group algorithm

Follow Sections 2–7 of `class7-rank2-proof.md`, replacing its rank-two
kernel arguments by Sections 3–4 above. There are two points to add.

First, leading degree four may now have type (2,2). Its independent
leading pair C,D is enumerated by the existing finite oriented-Hermite
procedure. Its degree-five correction is injective by map E in the
class-six proof. After incorporating that unique integral correction,
the tail corrections start in weights (4,4). Their cross term has
weight eight, and repeated-correction terms have weight at least ten.
The remaining degrees six and seven are therefore jointly linear.

Second, the weight-two Hall layer may have several generators.
For the one-dimensional affine kernel in (10), choose ordered Hall
lifts of v0+kK. Products or repeated powers of these weight-two
generators first introduce nonlinear terms in weight four. Combined
with the opposite base weight four they first affect the commutator
in weight eight. Thus the degree-seven residual is still an
integer-valued quadratic in the **single** integer k. Its possible
quadratic cross term comes from weights two and five. The final
fixed lattice is still [L3,D]+[z,L6], independent of k.

All other leading types, exact Nielsen residue normalizations, joint
linear tails, and the univariate polynomial-lattice decision are
unchanged. Each stage is finite and complete; positive instances give
exact commutator factors. The existing all-class degree-two algorithm
and penultimate/central algorithms handle the boundary strata.

## 6. Scope and dependencies

The resulting candidate algorithm covers every target in class seven,
in every finite rank. It does not cover all intermediate layers in
classes eight and above. It is the same single partial N8(b) candidate.

Besides the previously credited integral Hall theory and earlier N8
lemmas, the new structural dependency is the homogeneous Shirshov
lemma identified in Section 1. The kernel classification and its
application here are supplied in full; their novelty remains provisional.

Subsequent development: `class8-proof.md` extends the candidate to
class eight. This earlier proof and its original audit remain unchanged
in mathematical scope; the extension is still the same partial N8(b)
candidate.
