# Audit of the uniform third-layer extension

29 September2026. Candidate argument and implementation internally audited;
independent software replay completed. No outside specialist review.

## Exact scope and proof review

The new candidate covers every target of exact leading degree c-2 in
F_r/gamma_(c+1), in every finite rank and c>=6. Together with the earlier
central and penultimate algorithms it covers gamma_(c-2). All-target
classes3--10 and earlier arbitrary-class families are retained. Targets
in other lower layers of arbitrary class remain unresolved; N8 is still
one partial candidate, not a whole-entry answer. The standalone function
`decide_third_layer` explicitly returns None outside its own layer,
including the identity; the existing procedures handle those other
supported strata.

The complete frozen N8 page and linked background were reread, and the
actual original rendering viewed. Part(a)'s general class-two negative
result is separate from part(b)'s free-nilpotent question. The Hall
commutator convention is x^-1 y^-1 x y.

The central proof checks were:

1. The graded tail in Section1 really is a subalgebra, including p=1.
   C,U remain indecomposable in that subalgebra even when they are
   decomposable in L or L'. The homogeneous free-basis theorem is applied
   to this subalgebra. No false extension of the inner-solution theorem
   to arbitrary original coefficients is used.
2. For two independent first directions the same subalgebra contains
   C,U1,U2 as free basis elements. The intersection argument gives a
   one-dimensional upper bound for the fixed-D kernel, not for the space
   where D also varies.
3. The homogeneous projection of the final correction space is zero
   for p>=2. The p=1 exception is handled by the number of U letters,
   not by the false assertion that its degree3 projection vanishes.
4. Equal leading weights have zero first kernel. Adjacent leading
   weights have exactly the primitive Nielsen line, and every residue
   modulo content(D) is retained. These are separate from the nonzero
   quadratic-obstruction case.
5. The quadratic degree bound uses p+q=c-2. All higher Hall coordinates
   and correction interactions beyond class c are explicitly excluded
   by weight, not by bounded experiments. The final rational obstruction
   only restricts possible parameters; the full integral lattice still
   decides every surviving one.
6. The complete normalized leading-pair enumeration is unchanged from
   the earlier proof. No branch of first weight>=3 is discarded anymore.
   Its normalization/completeness remains a mathematical dependency;
   GAP does not independently enumerate every possible leading pair.

The exact primary Shirshov statement on Bryant--Kovacs--Stohr2005 p147
and Altassan2013 Theorem3.3 on p24 were viewed again. Their prior readings
and archives are retained. The latter's proof on pp24--25 was read in the
preceding work period; its general independent-coefficient discussion on
pp26--27 was reread during this derivation. The new argument uses the
strong free-basis statement, not merely the finite-codimension statement.
The Remeslennikov--Stohr2007 credit remains explicit. The case of a
subalgebra generated by only C,U is immediate; otherwise Theorem3.3
applies to its finite or countably infinite free basis.

Targeted searches for free-nilpotent commutator decision algorithms and
free-Lie inner solutions did not locate a matching uniform group-stratum
theorem. Related post-Lie results repeat the prior inner-solution theorem;
no novelty follows from that theorem itself. No exhaustive novelty audit
or current full-openness certification is claimed.

## Retained failed expectations

The initial class10 and rank3/class8 drivers wrongly expected a selected
first-layer perturbation of an equal-weight commutator to be negative.
The solver instead constructed factors. These are test-input expectations,
not assumptions of the proof or algorithm. Both runs stopped with exit1;
their exact executed drivers, completed prefixes and traceback witnesses
are retained in the unsuffixed certificate directories and v1 result logs.

The v2 driver names these simply first perturbations and expects the
observed positive result for equal weights. Independent GAP verifies
both explicit factors. The algorithm itself was unchanged. No failed
result or artifact was overwritten. The class11 run did not need rerunning.

## Weighted Lie checks of the new structural issue

`check_n8_third_structure.py` enumerates complete weighted Lyndon bases
and computes ranks over Q in the free associative algebra. A separate
GAP checker independently enumerates and brackets those bases in its
native free associative algebra; it does not import Python matrices.

Seven cases verify the first-kernel dimensions. Four exceptional cases
verify a nonzero final cokernel obstruction, with:

- C of weight3, U=[a,b] of weight4 (a,b both weight2);
- the same C and U=[a,b]+d with d an extra weight4 generator;
- C=[a,b] of weight4 and U=[a,t] of weight5 (t weight3);
- the same C and U=[a,t]+[b,t].

In each exceptional case D=ad_C^2(U) and V=[U,[C,U]]. The exact relation
[C,V]=[U,D] holds; the first kernel has dimension1; adjoining [U,V]
to the full final correction span increases its rank by1. Final span
ranks are27,54,92,92 respectively. The remaining controls have a generic
zero kernel, an adjacent-weight Nielsen kernel, and an equal-weight zero
kernel. This directly exercises decomposable coefficients that the old
free-seed argument did not address.

Both structural jobs passed with empty stderr in0.420 and2.878seconds.
They are finite weighted free-Lie controls, not computations in the whole
original rank-r free nilpotent group, and not a substitute for the uniform
proof. No all-rank conclusion is inferred merely from these dimensions.
