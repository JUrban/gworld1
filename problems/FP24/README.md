(FP24) (V.Guba) Let k[F] be the group ring of Thompson's group F
(see Problem (FP23)) over a field k. Does k[F] satisfy the Ore condition?
That is, for any a, b \in k[F], are there nonzero u, v \in k[F]
such that au = bv ? (If the answer is negative, then F is not
amenable.)
