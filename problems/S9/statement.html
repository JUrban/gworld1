*(S9) (B.Fine, V.Shpilrain) Let  u  be an element of a group  G.  We call  u  a test element if, whenever  f(u)=u  for some endomorphism  f  of the group G,  this   f  is actually an automorphism of G.  Does the free  solvable group of rank 2 and derived length d>2  have any test elements?   Background
