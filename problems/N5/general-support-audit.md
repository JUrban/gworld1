# Higher-class rational-support preimages for N5

30 September 2026. Following the [native Malcev input](general-malcev-input-audit.md),
the rational-support step of the existing general N5 candidate is now
implemented. The bounded-index search and complete central-lifting integration
are still separate. This does not change the result count or novelty assessment.

## Exact kernel construction

Let L be the rational Malcev Lie algebra of G/T(G), and let P be the sum of
any selected indecomposable centroid projections. Its image and the image of
1-P are commuting ideals. The map

    x -> ad((1-P)x)

is a Lie homomorphism. Indeed 1-P is the projection homomorphism to a direct
factor, followed by its inclusion, and ad is a Lie homomorphism. Its kernel
is im(P)+Z(L): an element in the complementary ideal acts trivially on all
of L exactly when it is central. The nilpotent BCH correspondence therefore
gives the group homomorphism

    G -> exp(ad((1-P) log(G/T(G)))).

Its kernel is precisely the preimage of R_I Z(R), in the notation of the
controlling proof. Map the kernel to K=G/Z(G) to obtain the required support
subgroup K_I. This retains finite torsion in K; it is not merely a rational
subspace intersection or a test of Hirsch lengths.

The program constructs the matrices exactly on native pcp generators. It
chooses a basis adapted to the decreasing spaces V, [L,V], [L,[L,V]], ...
for the adjoint action, with deepest spaces first. This makes the column
action matrices upper unitriangular. It then conjugates by

    D = diag(1,d,d^2,...,d^(h-1)),

where d is a common denominator of the generator matrices. Entry (i,j) of
D^-1 M D gains d^(j-i), so all upper entries become integral. This is
conjugation throughout; ordinary transpose would reverse multiplication.
The installed Polycyclic [matrix-to-pcp routine](https://gap-packages.github.io/polycyclic/doc/chap9.html)
converts the image to a pcp group, after which GAP calculates the kernel of
the generator-defined homomorphism. The trivial image is handled directly.

The implementation verifies centroid and idempotent conditions, upper
unitriangular form, integrality after conjugation, the resulting group map,
surjectivity onto the constructed image, and containment of Z(G) and T(G)
in the kernel. The matrix and pcp libraries remain trusted dependencies;
this is not formal verification of their algorithms.

## Controls and observed results

Twelve native groups cover forty partitions of the rational indecomposable
factors. Nine are the earlier Malcev-input controls. Three additional groups
are the product of the free rank-two class-three group, the Heisenberg group
and D8; a product of two copies of the class-three group; and the index-two
gluing below. Each rational decomposition is replayed by the native GAP
Lie/centroid/CRT checker before support calculations.

For every partition, the calculated subgroup is compared as an actual
subgroup of the same K with the one obtained from known native direct
factors, intersected with G where necessary, enlarged by Z and by T(K).
The complete sets of support subgroups agree. Zero projection gives T(K),
full projection gives K, and complementary supports intersect exactly in
T(K). In the D8-containing examples, |T(K)|=4. Denominator conjugations
actually use d=2 and d=12 in higher-class cases; this path is exercised.

For the negative control, take A free rank two of class three and B the
Heisenberg group. Write their first generators as a and b and let G be the
subgroup generated by ab, a^2, and every remaining pcp generator of both
factors. Its index in A x B is two, so its rational completion still splits
with Lie dimensions 5 and 3. Nevertheless the two proper complementary
support subgroups generate a subgroup of index two in G/Z(G). The program
retains this obstruction. This establishes failure of that integral support
partition, rather than asserting indecomposability of G from a rational test
alone. Empty/full partitions still have generation index one.
